Public Notice · No. 03 of 2026 Effective 19 August 2026

Appointment of the Mining Affairs Board

In terms of Section 7 of the Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05], the Ministry announces the appointment of the Mining Affairs Board with immediate effect, to strengthen oversight, transparency and accountability in the management of Zimbabwe’s mineral resources.
