About the ministry

Ministry of Mines and Mining Development

Unlocking Zimbabwe’s mineral wealth for sustainable growth and prosperity — building a strong, transparent and competitive mining sector.

“Sustainable Mining: Our Legacy”

On this page: Vision & missionMandateCore values Performance frameworkExplore the Ministry

Vision

A stable and sustainable mining industry by 2030.

Mission

To promote sustainable and responsible exploration, mining, beneficiation, value addition, marketing, and management of mineral resources.

Mandate

Overall Ministry functions

  1. Administer the Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05]
  2. Formulate, monitor and evaluate implementation of mining development policies
  3. Design mechanisms geared at effective accounting for the country’s mineral resources
  4. Administer and review mining and explosives laws
  5. Develop and maintain an up-to-date data bank on present/future mineral deposits
  6. Attract investment in the mining industry
  7. Promote beneficiation and value addition of mineral resources
  8. Promote and develop small-scale mining
  9. Facilitate involvement and participation of indigenous Zimbabweans in the mining sector
  10. Monitor and undertake exploration activities across the country
  11. Supervise and coordinate mining parastatals and state enterprises

Terms of reference

The purpose and mandate of the Ministry are set out in, amongst others:

  • The Constitution of Zimbabwe (No. 20 Act of 2013)
  • Statutory Instrument 206 of 2023
  • Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05]

What guides us

Core values

Transparency

We maintain an open-door policy and share relevant and accurate information with stakeholders.

Efficiency

We strive to deliver services in the least turnaround time as is possible within the confines of the law.

Teamwork

We believe results are best achieved through co-ordinated, motivated and competent teams working in mutual interdependence.

Accountability

We acknowledge and take responsibility for all actions and decisions within the scope of our mandate.

Integrity

We uphold the highest levels of honesty, sincerity and objectivity in all our operations and dealings.

Performance framework

What we measure ourselves against

Improved governance and administration
Increased mineral output
Improved environmentally sustainable mining
Increased exports of beneficiated minerals
