Step-by-step guide

How to get a mining claim

Requirements, forms, fees, where to apply and processing stages for registering a mining claim in Zimbabwe.

Check availability Download forms
Draft structure. Steps follow the Mines and Minerals Act and Client Service Charter standards. The Ministry will supply the approved procedure, forms and gazetted fees before publication.
1

Check availability

Confirm the ground is open for pegging on the Mining Cadastre portal, or ask the Provincial Mining Office.

Where: Online / PMO
Timeline: Same day
You need:
  • Mining Cadastre portal
2

Obtain a prospecting licence

Apply at your Provincial Mining Office with your identity document and the prescribed fee. Prospecting licences are issued on the date the application is received.

Where: Provincial Mining Office
Timeline: Same day
You need:
  • Prospecting licence application form
  • National ID / company registration
3

Peg the claim

Peg the claim on the ground in accordance with the Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05], displaying the prospecting licence number.

Where: On site
Timeline: —
You need:
  • Mines and Minerals Act, Part on pegging
4

Register the claim

Lodge the registration application and claim diagram at the Provincial Mining Office within the period set out in the Act, and pay the registration fee.

Where: Provincial Mining Office
Timeline: Determination within 3 months
You need:
  • Registration application form
  • Claim diagram / sketch plan
  • Registration fee
5

Inspection & survey

The Ministry may inspect the claim. Survey certificates are issued within 30 working days.

Where: PMO · Mining Cadastre
Timeline: 30 working days
You need:
  • Survey certificate
6

Certificate of registration

On approval, a certificate of registration is issued. You must then meet the ongoing obligations of a title holder.

Where: Provincial Mining Office
Timeline: Within 3 working days of approval
You need:
  • Certificate of registration
7

Stay compliant

Maintain inspection certificates, submit production returns, pay royalties and obtain environmental (EMA) approvals.

Where: PMO · EMA
Timeline: Ongoing
You need:
  • Production returns
  • EMA environmental approval

Requirements checklist

  • Valid national ID or company documents
  • Prospecting licence
  • Completed registration form
  • Claim diagram / sketch plan
  • Prescribed fees paid
  • EMA approval (before mining)

Fees

Prescribed fees are set by Statutory Instrument.

Gazetted fee schedule to be supplied

Where to apply

At the Provincial Mining Office for the province where the ground is located.
