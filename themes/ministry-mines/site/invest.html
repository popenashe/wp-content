The case

Why invest in Zimbabwe mining

World-class geology

The Great Dyke hosts the world’s second-largest platinum group metal reserves, alongside extensive gold greenstone belts.

Battery minerals

Africa’s leading lithium deposits, with active projects in Masvingo, Mashonaland and Matabeleland.

Clear title system

Mining rights are registered and verifiable through the national mining cadastre portal.

Investor support

The Ministry facilitates site visits, data access and introductions to provincial mining offices.
