Public Notice · No. 03 of 2026 Effective 19 August 2026

Appointment of the Mining Affairs Board

In terms of Section 7 of the Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05], the Ministry announces the appointment of the Mining Affairs Board to strengthen oversight, transparency and accountability.

Public Notice 02/2026 Effective immediately

Rotation and transfer of Provincial Mining Directors

To promote transparency, strengthen accountability and enhance service delivery, six Provincial Mining Directors have been rotated, including new postings to Matabeleland South, Mashonaland West, Masvingo, Mashonaland East and Matabeleland North.
