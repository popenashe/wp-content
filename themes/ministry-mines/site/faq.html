Frequently asked questions

Answers about mining claims, licences, service timelines, complaints and where to find the law.

How do I get a mining claim?+

Check availability on the Mining Cadastre, obtain a prospecting licence at your Provincial Mining Office (issued on the day you apply), peg the claim and lodge the registration application. See the step-by-step guide.

Source: Mining Cadastre · Read the guide

Where do I apply?+

Apply at the Provincial Mining Office for the province where the ground is located. Head-office services are provided from the four Harare centres.

Find your office

How long do services take?+

Prospecting licences are issued on the date of application; mineral export permits within 24 hours; laboratory results within 10 working days; and licences, permits and certificates within 3 working days.

All service standards

How do I lodge a complaint?+

Call the toll-free line 08004577 (landline only), email askminister@mines.gov.zw or use the online enquiry form. Complaints are acknowledged within 3 working days and investigated within 14 working days.

Send an enquiry

Where can I download the Mines and Minerals Act?+

The Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05], updated to 1 August 2021, is available in Resources together with the Explosives Act and policy documents.
